Paint and wallpaper can both give a room a new look. They behave differently on Dubai walls, though, and the better choice depends on the room, how long you plan to stay, and whether you own or rent. Here is how they compare.

## How Dubai's climate affects walls

Indoor conditions in Dubai are shaped by heavy AC use for most of the year. Rooms stay cool and dry while outside air is hot and, at certain times of year, very humid. This matters for wall finishes in a few ways:

- **Exterior facing walls** can be warmer and may collect condensation where cool indoor air meets them, particularly behind furniture.
- **Bathrooms and kitchens** see steam and splashes, so finishes need to tolerate moisture.
- **Rooms that are closed up**, for example while you travel in summer with the AC off, can get humid quickly.

Paint copes with these conditions more easily. Wallpaper can work well too, but it needs the right product and a properly prepared, dry wall.

## The case for paint

Paint is the default in most Dubai homes for good reasons.

- It is quick to apply and easy to touch up.
- Washable finishes such as satin or eggshell handle fingerprints and marks in busy areas.
- Moisture and mould resistant paints are available for bathrooms and kitchens.
- Repainting a room in a different colour is straightforward.

Paint also suits rentals. Many tenancy agreements expect walls to be returned in a neutral colour, and repainting is a simple job at move out. For more on choosing finishes, read our guide to [interior paint for Dubai's climate](/zaman/blog/interior-paint-dubai-climate/).

The main limit is texture and pattern. Paint effects exist, but a detailed pattern or a fabric look is far easier to achieve with wallpaper.

## The case for wallpaper

Wallpaper is popular for feature walls, bedrooms, majlis areas and offices where a stronger look is wanted.

- It offers patterns, textures and finishes that paint cannot easily match.
- Good quality wallpaper can hide minor surface imperfections better than flat paint.
- It can be very hard wearing, depending on type.

The downsides are cost per wall, the skill needed to hang it neatly, and removal. Wallpaper is also less forgiving of damp. If moisture gets behind it, seams can lift and mould can grow out of sight.

## Vinyl vs non-woven wallpaper

Two types are most common in homes.

### Vinyl wallpaper

Vinyl has a plastic surface layer, which makes it washable and durable. It is a practical choice for hallways, children's rooms and some kitchens. Because vinyl does not breathe well, the wall underneath needs to be dry and sound before hanging, or trapped moisture can cause problems.

### Non-woven wallpaper

Non-woven wallpaper is made from a blend of fibres. It is usually easier to hang, since the paste often goes on the wall rather than the paper, and it tends to come off more cleanly when removed. It allows some breathability, which helps on walls that are not perfectly dry.

Many products combine a non-woven backing with a vinyl surface, giving durability with easier installation and removal.

## Wall preparation makes the difference

Most wallpaper problems come from poor preparation, not the wallpaper. Before hanging:

1. Fix any damp first. Wallpaper over a damp wall will fail.
2. Fill cracks and holes and sand the wall smooth.
3. Remove flaking paint.
4. Apply a suitable primer or sealer so the paste bonds evenly and removal is easier later.

Paint needs similar preparation, but it is more forgiving if conditions are not ideal.

## Feature walls: combining both

A common approach in Dubai homes is to paint most walls and use wallpaper on one feature wall, such as behind a bed, a TV unit or a dining table. This keeps costs and maintenance down while still giving the room a focal point. Choose an interior wall away from bathrooms and exterior windows where possible.

## Wallpaper in rented homes

If you rent, check your tenancy contract and ask your landlord in writing before hanging wallpaper. Many landlords will ask for walls to be returned to their original condition, which means removal and repainting at move out. Non-woven or "paste the wall" wallpapers are easier to remove, but some repair and repainting is usually still needed. ## Choosing the right option

Choose paint for bathrooms, kitchens, rentals and rooms you may want to change often. Choose wallpaper for feature walls and dry rooms where you want pattern or texture and plan to keep the look for a while.
