Most Dubai apartments and villas come with tiled floors, and many owners later think about switching some rooms to wood. Both can work well. The right choice depends on the room, how it is used, and how much upkeep you want. The local climate matters more than in many other places, because heat, fine sand, humidity and constant AC all affect flooring.

## How Dubai's climate affects flooring

- **Heat and sunlight.** Strong sun through large windows can fade wood finishes and some laminates. Tiles are not affected.
- **Sand and dust.** Fine sand gets tracked in, especially in villas with gardens. On wood and laminate it acts like sandpaper and scratches the surface. Tiles resist scratching much better.
- **Humidity and AC.** Wood expands and shrinks with changes in moisture. Homes that are left closed in summer with the AC off can see big swings in humidity, which can cause gaps or cupping in wood floors.
- **Water.** Leaks, mopping and spills from AC units or kitchens are common. Tiles cope with water. Wood and laminate do not.

## Porcelain and ceramic tiles

### Pros

- Very hard-wearing and resistant to scratches from sand
- Not affected by water, so suitable for kitchens, bathrooms, balconies and laundry rooms
- Cool underfoot, which many people like in a hot climate
- Easy to clean with a mop
- Available in large formats and wood-look finishes

### Cons

- Hard and cold, which some people find less comfortable in bedrooms
- Can feel noisy, with sound echoing in larger rooms
- Grout lines need cleaning and can stain. See [whether to regrout or retile](/zaman/blog/regrout-or-retile-bathroom/) when grout starts to fail.
- Cracked tiles need careful replacement to match

**Porcelain** is denser and absorbs less water than standard **ceramic**, so it is the better choice for wet areas, high-traffic spaces and outdoor use.

## Wood flooring options

### Solid wood

Solid planks made from a single piece of timber. They feel warm and can be sanded and refinished several times. They are also the most sensitive to humidity and water, and need stable indoor conditions. In Dubai they are best kept to bedrooms and living rooms in homes where the AC runs consistently.

### Engineered wood

A real wood top layer bonded to a stable plywood or composite base. It looks like solid wood but handles changes in humidity better, which makes it a more practical choice for Dubai. Depending on the thickness of the top layer, it can be lightly sanded once or more over its life.

### Laminate

A printed image of wood under a hard wear layer, on a fibreboard core. It is usually the lowest-cost option, quick to install and fairly scratch-resistant. Its weakness is water: if water gets into the joints or the edges, the core can swell and does not recover.

## Cost factors to compare

Prices vary widely, so compare quotes on the same basis. Things that affect the final cost:

- **Material grade.** Thickness of the wear layer, tile quality and finish
- **Floor preparation.** Uneven screed must be levelled before wood or large tiles go down
- **Removing old flooring.** Laying over existing tiles is sometimes possible for floating floors, but adds height that can affect doors
- **Underlay and skirting.** Wood and laminate need underlay, and new skirting is often needed
- **Wastage and cuts.** Rooms with many corners use more material
- **Building rules.** Some apartment buildings require acoustic underlay to reduce noise for neighbours below

## Which flooring suits which room

- **Kitchens, bathrooms, laundry rooms:** porcelain tiles
- **Balconies and outdoor areas:** outdoor-rated porcelain with a slip-resistant finish
- **Living rooms:** tiles for durability, or engineered wood for warmth
- **Bedrooms:** engineered wood or good-quality laminate are both common choices
- **Entrances and areas near gardens:** tiles, because of sand
- **Rental properties:** tiles or durable laminate, since they handle tenant changeovers better

If you are planning a full refit, consider flooring alongside your [bathroom renovation](/zaman/blog/bathroom-renovation-planning-dubai/) or kitchen work so the levels and thresholds line up.

## When to call a flooring contractor

Call a contractor if your floor is uneven, tiles are cracking or sounding hollow, or you want to change flooring across several rooms. Good preparation of the base matters as much as the material.
